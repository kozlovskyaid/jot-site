Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This policy describes how Jot handles your information. Jot is built to work on your own devices: what you record stays with you.

What the App Works With

  • Voice recordings you make on your Apple Watch or iPhone.
  • The text of those recordings, produced by speech recognition.
  • The notes, reminders and topics you keep in the app.
  • Reminders you choose to add to Apple's Reminders app.
  • If you get Jot Pro: your purchase and whether it is active.

How It Is Used

Recordings are turned into text so they can be read and searched; the text is examined on your device to find dates, times and topics; notes with a date can be added to Reminders so you are alerted in time. Your purchase is checked so that Jot Pro works on your devices.

Where It Is Stored

Recordings, text, notes and topics are stored on your iPhone. A recording made on the watch travels from your watch to your iPhone over Apple's Watch Connectivity and is then removed from the watch.

Speech recognition runs on your device using Apple's Speech framework; the first time a language is used, the system may download that language's recognition model from Apple.

If you allow it, reminders are created in Apple's Reminders app, where they are handled by Apple (and synced through your iCloud account if you use it) under Apple's own privacy policy.

Jot has no account and no server of its own. To sell Jot Pro and confirm who has it, Jot uses RevenueCat, a service that processes in-app purchases. When the app starts, it asks RevenueCat whether Jot Pro is active, using a random identifier the app creates — not your name, email address or Apple Account. The app keeps this identifier in your iCloud Keychain, so Jot Pro stays with you when you reinstall the app or use another of your devices; Settings shows it as your Jot ID. If you buy Jot Pro, RevenueCat receives the purchase record from the App Store. With these requests RevenueCat also receives basic technical details such as the app and iOS versions, the country of your App Store account and your IP address. RevenueCat handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Your recordings, their text, your notes and your topics are never sent to RevenueCat or anyone else. Payment itself is handled by Apple; Jot never sees your payment details.

Your Controls

Edit or delete any note in the app; delete a note's recording together with it; switch adding to Reminders off in Settings; change microphone, speech recognition and Reminders access in iOS Settings; manage or cancel a Jot Pro subscription in your Apple Account settings; uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will appear in the app and on this website with a new effective date.
