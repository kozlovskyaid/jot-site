How do I start recording as fast as possible?

Opening Jot on your Apple Watch starts recording straight away. To get there in one tap, add the “Record a Note” complication to your watch face (touch and hold the face → Edit → Complications → Jot), or add the “Record a Note” button to Control Center on the watch — on Apple Watch Ultra it can also go on the Action button. You can also say “Record with Jot” to Siri.

Does my iPhone need to be nearby?

No. The watch keeps the recording and sends it to your iPhone as soon as they are connected again. The watch shows how many recordings are still waiting.

Which languages does Jot understand?

English and Russian. Settings → Speech → Language picks one, or follows your device's language. The first time a language is used, iOS may download its speech model once.

How does Jot decide a note is a task?

If you name a day or a time — “tomorrow”, “on Wednesday”, “at 8 pm”, “in two hours” — the note becomes a task for that moment. Things to do without a date, like “need to call the dentist”, go to Keep in mind. Everything else is kept as a note. You can change the type, the date and the topics of any note by tapping it.

Why is there a reminder in Apple's Reminders app?

Tasks with a date are added to Reminders so they alert you on all your devices, even when Jot is closed. You can switch this off in Settings.

A note says “Couldn't make out the words”.

The recording is kept. Tap “Try Again”, check the language in Settings, or open the note and type the text yourself.

How do topics work?

Every topic has words. A note joins a topic when it contains one of its words, in any form; the topic with the most matches is the main one. Add, rename and recolour topics in Topics (the tag button).

How do I delete a note?

Swipe it to the left in the planner, or open it and choose Delete Note. The recording is deleted with it.
